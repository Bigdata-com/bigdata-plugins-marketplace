## Literature verdicts (checked against the texts in ~/Downloads/investing-resources)

- D1 Steady-state value. Mauboussin and Callahan, "What Does a P/E Multiple Mean?" (2014), p. 4: the perpetuity is NOPAT / cost of capital; excess cash sits outside it and the equity bridge adds it once. The library had it inside the division and added it again. Fixed in expectations.md. The paper's own layout is ambiguous on the page, so I followed the economics.
- D2 Net capex. The UBS DCF papers do not define "net capex = change in net PP&E + depreciation"; that is gross capex. Fixed in valuation.md: capex = change in net PP&E + depreciation; net investment = change in net PP&E.
- D3 Stock compensation. Berkowitz (Security Analysis 6th ed., pp. 341-342) builds free cash flow from net income and complains that options were not expensed. Since 2006 (ASC 718) they are, so deducting SBC again double counts. Fixed in earnings-quality.md formula 8.
- D4 ROIIC against WACC. Mauboussin (Calculating ROIC, 2014, p. 13) says one-year ROIIC is noisy and to use rolling averages; Damodaran (2007, p. 51) says marginal returns swing. Expectations Investing uses incremental return against the cost of capital for value triggers. Reconciled: the trigger test uses a three-year rolling ROIIC, labelled; a single year is never evidence. Changed roic.md and expectations.md; the SKILL.md bodies that cite the rule get the same wording in the per-skill pass.
- D5 Credit ratios. McKinsey 5th ed. uses EBITA/interest and the 1981-2003 S&P default table; Mauboussin's 2016 exhibit 14 gives 2011-13 medians (the one-year default rates are 2014, correctly labelled; a reviewer was wrong there). Neither source has the agencies' post-2013 core ratios. I added table 3 to credit.md with S&P's FFO/debt and debt/EBITDA ranges from its 2013 Corporate Methodology, marked as outside the folder and to verify, because a credit note without them is not professional. Alternative: leave the file on the folder's sources only and say the ratios are missing.
- D6 Merger arbitrage. Klarman annualises returns by holding period (ch. 12) and hedges the Burlington Northern pair (ch. 10) but gives no carry formula. I added carry, the stock-deal hedge and the annualised-spread comparison as "standard practice, not in the sources" in special-situations-arithmetic.md.
- D7 Operating cash allowance. Four conventions were in the library. Unified to Mauboussin's 2 percent of sales (5 percent for less predictable companies), which McKinsey ch. 7 matches at 2 percent. The unsourced "2 to 4 percent, rating-agency rule of thumb" is gone.
- D8 Refinancing stress and structural subordination were added to credit.md as "not in the sources"; the folder has nothing on them and a 2026 credit note needs both.
- D9 Software primer: CAC payback formula now multiplies by 12 (months); the R&D tax line now states the 2022-24 amortisation and the July 2025 restoration of expensing (from my own knowledge, cutoff mid-2026; marked verify). Banks primer gained uninsured-deposit share, LCR/NSFR, unrealised-loss share of CET1 and CRE concentration rows, all marked judgment or verify.
- D10 Dated tables (S&P 1981-2003 defaults, HOLT 2016 profit pools, 2014 compensation survey) stay, with a stronger label that they are historical and the order of magnitude only. Replacing them needs sources that are not in the folder.
