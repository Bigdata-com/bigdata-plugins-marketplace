## Thesis-status vocabulary

When the user supplied a thesis, replace the two-sided bull/bear check with an explicit status: **Intact / Strengthened / Weakened / Broken**, followed by the specific data points from the quarter that support it.

## Footer rule

The **Powered by Bigdata.com** line and the **Disclaimer** block above are mandatory and must be reproduced **verbatim**, after the Sources section, in every user-facing deliverable.
