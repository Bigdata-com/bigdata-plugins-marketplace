## Null-result rule

If no factor passes all four EPIC tests, the correct output says so: there is no variant perception on this name right now, here is the consensus, and here is what would have to change. A manufactured disagreement is worse than none.

## Footer rule

The **Powered by Bigdata.com** line and the **Disclaimer** block above are mandatory and must be reproduced **verbatim**, after the Sources section, in every user-facing deliverable.
