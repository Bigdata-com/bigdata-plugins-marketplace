# Bigdata.com Research

Part of the **Bigdata.com** plugin.

The entry point to Bigdata.com: research any company, security, sector, country, market or economy with Bigdata.com data, and turn a task you repeat into a skill of your own. Powered by **Bigdata.com MCP**.

---

## What It Does

- Answers any financial research task with cited Bigdata.com data
- Explains what data Bigdata.com has, with an example request for each
- Builds your own skill from a task you repeat, one question at a time

---

## Usage

Ask in natural language, or type:

    /bigdata
